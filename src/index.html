Route 9 · Ashby Hollow, Kentucky Night 1

Kessler Farm

Forty acres of soybean and rust off Route 9, three miles outside Ashby Hollow, Kentucky. The radio stopped making sense on Tuesday. By Thursday the Kesslers were gone, and the people who used to wave from passing trucks had started walking the fence line instead.

Board up. Prepare for the horde. Survive as long as you can.

Zombies drop scrap; the workbench turns it into ammo and upgrades for your weapons. Survive a wave and you take one find: a skill, a perk, some aid, or a better gun. When you die, the farm is theirs.
